Eclipse Shøp
Based on mvShop v1.0.6 by mvfsullivan, updated and rebranded as Eclipse Shøp.

Setup:
- Money is stored on the "money" scoreboard (same as mvShop, so balances carry over).
- New players get an Eclipse Shøp book the first time they join.
- The book can also be crafted: 3 paper + 1 book (paper, paper / paper, book),
  or given with: /give @s coded:shop
- Change the shop name, scoreboard, currency symbol and first-join book at the
  top of scripts/index.js. Prices are in scripts/items.js.
- Pick the sell percent (10-100% of the buy price) in the pack's settings.
